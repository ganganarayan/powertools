Service Delivery Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The Power Tools for Coaches suite by DivineLeads consists entirely of digital software services. There is no physical product and therefore no physical shipping.

1. Digital delivery

  • Access to each tool is provided electronically through your online account at powertools.divineleads.guru and related domains.
  • On successful sign-up, free access is provisioned immediately.
  • On a successful paid upgrade, the corresponding features are activated on your account, typically instantly and in any case within 24 hours of payment confirmation.

2. Delivery confirmation

You will receive confirmation by email and/or in-app once your account or upgrade is active. If you do not receive access within 24 hours of payment, contact us and we will resolve it promptly.

3. No physical shipping

Because all products are delivered digitally, no shipping charges, couriers, or delivery timelines for physical goods apply.

4. Access requirements

To use the Services you need a compatible device, a modern web browser, and an internet connection. For tools that connect third-party services (cloud providers, SMTP servers, payment gateways), you are responsible for maintaining those accounts.

5. Help with access

If you have any trouble accessing a purchased feature, email connect@divineleads.guru with the tool name in the subject line, or WhatsApp 9356819176.
