This Cookie Policy explains how DivineLeads uses cookies and similar technologies on the Power Tools for Coaches websites and Services.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and understand how it is used.

2. Types we use

  • Strictly necessary — required for core functions such as signing in, security, and remembering your session. These cannot be switched off.
  • Preference — remember choices such as language or display settings.
  • Analytics — help us understand usage so we can improve the Services. These may be set by us or trusted analytics providers.
  • Marketing — where used, these measure and improve advertising, including ads that bring you to this site. They are set only where permitted.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties we use for analytics, advertising, embedded media, or payments. Their use of cookies is governed by their own policies.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and where we present a cookie banner you can adjust non-essential categories. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Services work.

5. Changes

We may update this policy as our use of cookies evolves. Please check this page periodically.
